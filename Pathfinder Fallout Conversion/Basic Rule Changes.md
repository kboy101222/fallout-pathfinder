The basic 6 stats are changed to SPECIAL
* STR, CHR, and INT stay the same
* DEX is now AGI
* PER is removed as a skill and is instead an ability
* CON is now END
* LCK carries its own rules

Skills by SPECIAL Stat:
STR:
* Athletics
* Melee Weapons
PER:
* Energy Weapons
* Explosives
* Lockpick
END:
* Survival
* Unarmed
CHR:
* Barter
* Speech
INT:
* Medicine
* Repair
* Science
AGI:
* Sneak
* Guns
* Acrobatics
LCK:
* None

SPECIAL stats have a minimum of 1 and a maximum of 10 from skill points, though items and abilities are not bound to these restrictions. At character creation, all SPECIAL stats are set to 1, and the player recieves 40 points to divide as they wish amongst their SPECIAL stats, increasing a stat by 1 per point spent.

Each SPECIAL provides a bonus to rolls equal to half of its value rounded down, so +0 at 1, +1 at 2-3, +2 at 4-5, etc.

Hero Points are now Luck Points (LP). Your maximum Luck point total is 2 + LCK, to a maximum of 5 (tho perks and feats may raise this cap). You start each game with half your Luck Points available (rounded up), and you gain an LP each time you roll a critical hit on a roll, and an additional LP whenever you roll a 20 on a die. 

Classes are represented by roles within the wasteland, such as Sniper, Soldier, Assassin, Medic, Scientist, Chemist, and whatever else I can think of. 

Ancestries are represented by the different species, such as Humans, Synths, Super Mutants, Robots, and Ghouls. Ancestries still provide boosts and negatives to SPECIALS

Might not include backgrounds, idk. Classes in the wasteland represent these better, but I may include them as some more minor/non-adventuring roles in the wasteland, such as Farmer, Reporter, Ex-Slave, Faction Exile, etc.

Reputation with factions determines bonuses to rolls when interacting with their respective members. Reputation is calculated for each player, with party interactions being the average reputation score among all members present within the scene.

Players may take up to 2 Traits at character creation. These each provide a benefit and a negative side effect.

Armor provides 2 stats - Armor Class (AC) and Damage Threshold (DT). AC is the minimum value needed to hit a body part or the character in general. DT is a reduction of damage applied when hit.

When a character, NPC or PC, make an attack, they choose to either make a general attack or a precision attack. When they make a general attack, AC and DT is determined using the chest armor. When they make a targeted attack, the attack itself takes a status penalty to the attack depending on the body part. Then, choose a body part (head, body, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg, left hand, right hand). If the attack hits, consult the following table for the bonus effect. All weapons and damage types gain the effects of the Any damage type in addition to any other effects.

On a critical hit, increase any persistent damage or condition values or duration by 1 (so 1 Persistent becomes 2, Stupified 1 becomes Stupified 2, Dazzled for 1 turn becomes Dazzled for 2 turns, etc)

### **Disarm**
When you make a Precision Strike against a target's hands, if they are carrying an item in that hand, you may attempt to disarm them. Exactly what is rolled depends on the type of weapon used:

**Melee & Unarmed** The target makes a Reflex save versus your Athletics DC. 

**Critical Failure**
* If the item in the target's hand is a weapon or shield, it gains the Broken condition. Then, the target drops the item in a square within 5 feet of them.
* If the item in the target's hand is not a weapon, the target drops the item within 10 feet of them.
**Failure** The target drops the item within 5 feet of them.
**Success** The target may not use the item until the beginning of their next turn.
**Critical Success** No effect.
