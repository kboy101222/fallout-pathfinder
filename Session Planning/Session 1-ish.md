Rae, you find yourself in one of Gulch Town's doctors' stations, treating a sick patient. Work for you has been especially busy lately, with several patients currently waiting outside the door. However, you find yourself unable to to focus on the waiting patients. This man on your table had been screaming and writhing in pain mere moments ago, but quickly went silent and stiff. Though his heart is still beating, you know from experience that you have but mere moments to figure out how to treat this to slow its progression, if you can even figure out what it is.
Make an **INT + Medicine** check:
* **2 Successes**: Using your extensive knowledge of Wasteland medicine, you mix a cocktail of medicines together and inject it into the man. You hold your breath for a moment as his body doesn't react, before letting out a sigh of relief as his once tense and paralyzed body relaxes. However, it's impossible to know which of the medicines helped without further study. But for now, the patient will live
	* *This outcome will require a further check in the future to help determine a proper cure without having to spend additional medical supplies.*
* **3 Successes**: You pause to think for a moment to catch your breath. You've read about this before. It's a disease referred to in Athenian Scripture as Cave Rot - a disease that slowly turns joints to stone. It's not a common disease, but in times of being better supplied, the cure was simple and made of common ingredients - a 1-to-1 mixture of boiled cave cricket blood and river lilies. In these times however, your supplies are extremely limited. Digging through your medical bag, you pull out a small pouch of dried river lily. Taking a sniff, its potency has worn off over time, but you quickly mix what you have with some alcohol and inject it into the patient. Moments later, the patient's previously paralyzed body begins relaxing. They'll live for the moment, but what you've given them will only treat the major symptoms for a few days at most.
